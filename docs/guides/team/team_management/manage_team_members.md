# Manage Team Members

This section describes how to view, invite, and remove members of your workspace. Only the workspace owner can invite or remove members.

## Open the Team Page

Click your avatar in the upper-right corner, open **User settings**, then select **Team** in the left sidebar.

![Enter The Team Page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/infiniflow/ragflow-docs/main/images/enter_the_team_page.jpg)

Each account has its own workspace. The page title shows your nickname followed by **workspace**.

The page contains two lists:

- **Team members** shows people you have invited to your workspace and those who have joined. Your own account is not listed as a member.
- **Joined teams** shows your own workspace, other teams you have joined, and invitations awaiting your response. Your own workspace has no leave action.

Joining another team does not switch **Team members** to that team's member list. To respond to another owner's invitation or leave their team, see [Join and Leave Teams](join_and_leave_teams.md).

## View Members

In **Team members**, review the people you have invited and those who have joined.

![View Current Workspace Members](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/infiniflow/ragflow-docs/main/images/view_current_workspace_members.jpg)

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Name | The member's nickname. |
| Date | The last update time for the member's account, not the date they were invited or joined. |
| Email | The member's email address. |
| State | **Invite** means the invitation is awaiting a response. **Member** means the person has joined. |
| Action | The delete icon opens a confirmation to remove the member or cancel a pending invitation. |

Use the search box beside **Invite member** to search by nickname or email address. Click the sort button beside **Date** to change the order.

To view invitations from other owners or teams you have joined, see [View Joined Teams](join_and_leave_teams.md#view-joined-teams).

## Invite Members

Invite another registered RAGFlow user to collaborate on resources you share with your team.

1. In **Team members**, click **Invite member**.

   ![Invite Members](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/infiniflow/ragflow-docs/main/images/invite_members_1.jpg)

2. In the **Add** dialog, enter the user's email address.
3. Click **Ok**.

![Invite Members](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/infiniflow/ragflow-docs/main/images/invite_members_2.jpg)

The invited person appears with the **Invite** state until they accept. The recipient must [accept the invitation](join_and_leave_teams.md#accept-or-decline-an-invitation) from their own account. The state then changes to **Member**.

To share resources with your members, see [Resource Sharing and Permissions](../resource_sharing/resource_sharing_and_permissions.md).

### If the Invitation Fails

Check that the email belongs to a registered account and that the person is not already a member or awaiting an invitation response.

## Remove Members or Cancel Invitations

As the workspace owner, you can remove a member or cancel an invitation that has not yet been accepted.

1. Find the person in **Team members** and check their email address.
2. Click the delete icon in **Action**.

   ![Remove Members](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/infiniflow/ragflow-docs/main/images/remove_members.jpg)

3. In **Delete member**, click **Delete** to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep the person in the list.

Removing a member ends their access to resources shared only with your team. It does not delete their account or their personal resources. You can invite them again later if needed.

Your own account is not listed as a removable member of your workspace.
